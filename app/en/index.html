Reinforced soil wall

Overall stability, rupture and pull-out, sliding, bearing, overturning and eccentricity of a wall with a vertical face, in 1st or 2nd generation.

Not covered: inclined face, reinforced slopes and base reinforcement, reinforcements of variable length, water table in the block, seismic action, serviceability limit states.

Geometry (m)

The layers are computed from the base up to H at spacing Sv; their number is read from the results. The length is uniform over all the layers.

Foundation soil

Undrained: cu,k replaces φ’k and c’k in the foundation calculation.

Surcharges on the crest (kPa)

Uniform over the whole crest, above both the block and the retained fill. The variable one is not counted in the resistances (pull-out, sliding).

Reinforcement
Corrosion (EN 1997-3, 9.3.4)
Coherent gravity (F.3.2)

Default values: FHWA-NHI-10-024 (μpo,0 = min(2; 1.2 + log Cu), Cu = 4; μpo,z0 = tan φ’r). EN 1997-3 leaves their determination to tests or to comparable experience.

The line of maximum tension becomes the Rankine wedge (F.3.3), at 45° − φ’/2; the factor ηgs groups the conversion factors.

General stability circle

The search keeps only the circles that exit at the toe or in front: a circle exiting through the face would cut a slice held by the face, outside the model.

Generation and factors

With ANB:2022, the approach is fixed to DA1 (greyed list) and the risk (low, normal, high) is taken into account. The two divisors block the calculation as long as they are empty: neither EN 1997-1:2004 nor the ANB gives these factors.

The 2nd generation offers the consequence class, the external case, then the internal approach: VC3 + M2 by materials, VC1 by resistances (EN 1997-3, table 9.4).

Switching generation keeps the data.
